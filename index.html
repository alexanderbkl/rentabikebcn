How it works

From phone to pedals in four steps.

  1. Choose your ride

    City bike, e-bike, scooter or mountain bike — pick what fits your plan.

  2. Message us

    Check availability on WhatsApp, or simply walk in — both shops are open daily.

  3. Pick up & get set

    Leave one ID document or cash as a deposit. We fit your helmet and lock and adjust the seat.

  4. Ride & return

    Explore at your own pace and bring the bike back to the shop at the agreed time.

Good to know

Questions? We've got answers.

What do I need to rent a bike?

Just one ID document or cash as a deposit. A helmet and a lock are included with every rental.

Do I need to book in advance?

You can simply walk in, but in busy season it's smart to message us on WhatsApp to check availability and today's prices.

Which bike is best for me?

City bikes are ideal for the centre and the beach. Choose an e-bike for longer distances and hills, or a mountain bike for Montjuïc and park trails.

Can I get a basket or a baby seat?

Yes — baskets and baby seats are available. Let us know when you message us.

Can I just leave my luggage?

Absolutely. We store bags and suitcases of any size under video surveillance, even if you are not renting a bike.

What are your opening hours?

Both shops are open every day from 10:00 to 19:00.

Ready to ride Barcelona?

Message us now and your bike will be waiting. Helmet and lock on the house.
